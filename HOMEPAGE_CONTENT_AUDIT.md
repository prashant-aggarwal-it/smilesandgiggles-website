# Homepage Content Audit — Streamlined Structure

## Why the homepage was feeling too long

The previous homepage rendered 16 separate sections. Several of those sections repeated information that now has a stronger dedicated destination elsewhere on the site. The homepage had gradually become a compressed version of About, Our Work, Impact and CSR Partnerships rather than a fast orientation page.

## New homepage role

The homepage now does four jobs only:

1. Explain what the Foundation does.
2. Establish credible proof quickly.
3. Route visitors to the right area of the website.
4. Offer clear ways to partner, participate or donate.

## Sections retained on the homepage

1. Hero — immediate proposition and primary actions.
2. Impact Areas Rail — fast routing to STEM, AI/Digital, Health, CSR and Get Involved.
3. Impact at a Glance — concise proof points.
4. Three Core Areas — substantive overview of the Foundation's work.
5. Learning in Action — visual proof / gallery gateway.
6. CSR Partnership Gateway — why an institutional partner can work with the Foundation.
7. Governance & Compliance — compact trust signal.
8. Final Action CTA — Get Involved, CSR Partnerships and Donate in one place.

## Sections removed from homepage rendering

### Our Flagship Focus
Moved conceptually to `/our-work/stem-education`. The homepage already presents STEM as one of the core work areas, so a second large STEM explanation was repetitive.

### Our Story
Moved to `/about`. Detailed organizational origin and narrative belong on About, while the homepage only needs the mission/value proposition.

### Implementation Model
Moved to `/csr-partnerships`. The delivery process is valuable for institutional decision-makers but too detailed for general homepage visitors.

### Beyond the Numbers / Impact Outcomes
Moved to `/impact`. The homepage retains high-level impact statistics and directs deeper evidence to the Impact page.

### Government Engagement
Moved to `/our-work/stem-education` and `/impact`. MCD and Vidyanjali evidence remains important but does not need another full homepage section.

### CSR Support Options
Moved to `/csr-partnerships`. Partnership models, kits, infrastructure and teacher enablement are decision-stage content.

### Field Stories
Moved to `/impact#field-evidence`. The homepage already has a visual Learning in Action gallery; both sections served a similar proof role.

### Standalone Donation Strip
Merged into the final action CTA. Donate remains visible in site navigation and now appears alongside Get Involved and CSR Partnerships at the end of the homepage.

## Result

Homepage sections reduced from 16 to 8 — approximately a 50% reduction in major section count, while preserving the important content elsewhere on the website.
