# Homepage Final Pass

This revision finalizes the public homepage as a quick, visual overview rather than a condensed copy of every internal page.

## Final homepage journey

1. Hero — philosophy, audience and two actions
2. Five-pathway navigation rail
3. Our Reach — four proof points / headline statistics
4. Our Work — three balanced core areas
5. Learning in Action — four visual contexts: STEM, digital, health, showcase
6. CSR & Institutional Partnerships — six implementation strengths
7. Trust & Transparency — governance indicators
8. Take Part — Get Involved / CSR / Donate

## Editorial rules applied

- Eyebrows add context instead of repeating the heading.
- Detailed implementation, stories, curricula and support models stay on dedicated pages.
- Homepage copy is kept short enough to scan without prior knowledge.
- Section backgrounds alternate intentionally so adjacent sections do not visually merge.
- Homepage vertical spacing uses dedicated compact spacing instead of the site's larger generic `section-pad`.
- No public development/placeholder labels are shown.
