# Get Involved Page — Design & Content Notes

Route: `/get-involved`

## Purpose

Convert interest into practical participation without duplicating the dedicated Donate and CSR Partnership pages.

## User pathways

1. Give Time — volunteer around real program needs.
2. Share Skills — skill-based mentoring or pro-bono contribution.
3. Connect a School — refer a school, NGO, community centre or institution.
4. Support a Project — support practical learning, skills or preventive-awareness needs.
5. Donate — route to the dedicated Razorpay donation page.

## Page flow

Hero → Interactive pathway chooser → Volunteer roles + form → Skill Match → School/community referral → Practical support areas → Corporate employee volunteering → Safeguarding → Closing CTA

## Form behaviour

The volunteer and school-referral forms currently prepare a structured email to the Foundation using the visitor's local email application. No fake server-side success state is shown. A future form service can replace the submission function without redesigning the page.

## Safeguarding rule

Any activity involving children, schools or health work remains subject to role suitability, permissions, supervision, verification and professional qualifications where relevant.

## CMS later

Keep the action-pathway copy, volunteer roles, skill mapping and support areas as structured content. Keep the forms and layout in code.
