# Temporary illustrative image placeholders

This build uses temporary AI-generated visuals only to establish layout, crop, age mix and context until approved authentic Smiles & Giggles Foundation photographs are supplied.

## Important publishing rule

These visuals are temporary design images and must be replaced with approved authentic Foundation photography before final public launch. Their temporary status is tracked internally in this file rather than displayed as development labels on the public website.

Before final public launch, replace them with approved authentic photographs and keep the same aspect ratios wherever possible.

- `stem-community-younger.webp` — younger learners, mixed attire, modest community-school setting
- `stem-younger-maroon.webp` — younger learners, different school uniform, simple STEM builds
- `stem-casual-workshop.webp` — casual-clothes community learning / foundational STEM
- `robotics-secondary.webp` — older students, different uniform, robotics/electronics

- `science-fair-clean-energy.webp` — different-school clean-energy exhibition
- `clean-energy-project.webp` — clean-energy project presentation with another uniform style
- `inclusive-community-stem.webp` — mixed-attire community STEM learning group

## Visual safeguards used

- Indian context throughout
- varied ages and school/community settings
- varied uniforms and casual clothing so the site does not look like one school
- positive lower-income / government-school visual cues without depicting children as destitute
- full faces kept inside the frame with safe margins
- no government or school logos presented as endorsement
